About Mo – The Man Behind Lakeside Picnic

Hi, I’m Mo.

If you’ve been to Lakeside Picnic, you’ve probably seen me behind the counter—serving you ice cream, pulling a coffee shot, helping you pick out the perfect gift—or just offering a warm hello. But what you may not know is why I’m here, and the journey that brought me to this place.

From the Ground Up

I didn’t come from much. I actually started my life without a home. My parents divorced and each started new families—I was left in the middle, and by 15, I found myself living alone in my grandparents’ old, empty house. I worked to survive, paid for my own school, and somehow managed to finish my university degree in commerce while working full-time in the computer and tech world.

At 21, I opened my first internet café in Egypt using a mix of savings and a loan. I grew it fast—within a year I had over 100 customers connected through my network. I paid off the loan, reinvested, and opened a bigger café, then a full tech shop at 25.

The Journey to the UK

My move to the UK started unexpectedly. A British manager working in Egypt came to me for help recovering data from her laptop. I fixed it. We stayed in touch. We became partners. After three years in Egypt, she encouraged me to come to the UK.

I left behind everything I’d built, and I started again from zero.

I worked in shops, restaurants, petrol stations—even a Michelin-starred kitchen. I didn’t just work—I learned. I took food safety training seriously, earning certifications yearly—even when not required—because I believe my customers deserve safe, high-standard service. I learned the UK hospitality industry from the ground up.

Now I’m a Father Too

I promised myself long ago that I wouldn’t bring children into this world unless I could give them everything I never had. And I kept that promise.

Today, I’m proud to say I have two amazing children—9 and 7 years old—and every day, I do my best to raise them with the values life taught me: strength, honesty, kindness, and hard work. I don’t just want to give them a better life—I want to teach them how to live it well.

What I Stand For

  • Serving the best possible products at a fair price
  • Being real—no fake smiles, no tricks, just genuine care
  • Respecting everyone equally—no judgment, no assumptions
  • Quality over profit—always

If you’re not happy with something, you get your money back. That’s my personal promise.

I’ve lived in Waterhead for over 15 years—longer than any other business owner around here. While businesses around me have changed hands, I’ve been here, growing, learning, and showing up every day with passion and purpose.

One Final Thing

I’m not for sale. My products are.

If you’re coming here just to ask personal questions or out of curiosity about me, that’s okay—but I’d rather you come because you want the best. Because that’s what I strive to give—the best ice cream, the best coffee, the best service, the best experience—with honesty and heart behind every single detail.

And if something’s not right? We’ll fix it—no questions asked. That’s the Lakeside Picnic promise.

Thank you for reading.
Thank you for visiting.
And thank you for giving my little business—and my big dream—a place in your day.

— Mo

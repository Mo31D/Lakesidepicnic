About Lakeside Picnic

Small beginnings. Big heart. Timeless quality.

Our Story

Located on the shores of Lake Windermere in Waterhead, Ambleside, Lakeside Picnic is a small, independent shop built from the ground up with hard work, resilience, and a true passion for quality.

When we first opened our doors in March 2015, we started with just a coffee machine, an ice cream freezer, and a dream: to create a welcoming space where visitors and locals could relax, enjoy beautiful surroundings, and discover some of the Lake District’s finest local treats.

We didn’t start with much — no big investment, no fancy setup — just a commitment to doing things the right way. Every bit of profit in those early years was carefully reinvested, not to chase quick success, but to build a place that genuinely served the needs of the community.

Listening and Growing

Rather than guessing what people might want, we listened carefully. When customers asked for postcards, we found the best ones. When they needed maps or local specialties like Romney’s Kendal Mint Cake, we brought them in. Slowly and thoughtfully, Lakeside Picnic became a true reflection of what people were looking for: authentic local products, honest service, and a warm, friendly experience.

Resilience at the Heart

Like many businesses in the Lake District, we faced hard challenges along the way. In our very first year, Storm Desmond devastated the area, and our shop was completely flooded. We lost almost everything — but not our determination. Having started from scratch once before, we simply rolled up our sleeves and did it again. Long working hours, multiple jobs, and unwavering commitment allowed us to rebuild stronger than ever.

Today

Today, Lakeside Picnic stands as a testament to resilience and dedication. We may be a small, seasonal business, but we've endured where many others have not. While shops, hotels, and businesses around us have changed ownership or disappeared over the years, we’ve remained — a steady presence in the heart of the Lake District.

Our success isn’t measured by size, but by our ability to keep true to our values and our community.
We’ve worked hard to stay personal, focused, and committed to the people who matter most — our customers. In an industry where businesses come and go, we’ve built a reputation for quality, honesty, and care that we’re proud to pass on for generations.

We’re not here to chase profit or become a bigger name. We’re here to remain a trusted part of the Lake District for years to come — one that is built to last, with heart at its core.

The Lakeside Picnic Promise

We are extremely selective about what goes into our small shop. With limited space, only the best deserves a place here — products chosen not because they are easy to sell, but because they truly reflect the spirit and quality of the Lake District.

At Lakeside Picnic, you’ll find:

  • Award-winning Luxury Lakes Ice Cream made in Kendal, using fresh, local ingredients
  • Freshly brewed coffee and light bites
  • A carefully chosen selection of Lake District gifts and local treats
  • A refreshing range of soft drinks, including Coca-Cola, Fanta, Sprite, and more – perfect for quenching your thirst
  • Friendly, knowledgeable staff providing expert service with a personal touch – no pressure, just great advice
The Person Behind the Counter

Visitors often notice the person serving them behind the counter — Mo, the founder of Lakeside Picnic (and now The Black Sheep Shop, also in Ambleside).

Mo’s journey is one of resilience, hard work, and dedication to honest business. After years of working in local shops, restaurants, hotels, and even Michelin-starred kitchens, he poured everything he learned into building Lakeside Picnic — one small, careful step at a time.

He prefers not to make the business about personal stories. For Mo, what matters most is the experience you have:
The quality of what you taste, the honesty of the service you receive, and the welcome you feel.

It’s not about where someone came from — it’s about what they stand for today. And here at Lakeside Picnic, we stand for quality, fairness, and genuine care.

Welcome
